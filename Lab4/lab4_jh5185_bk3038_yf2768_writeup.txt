1. In the bottom of the board there is a layout of MOSI and MISO that explains which GPIO # it is associated to. 
   In the accelerometor, or the slave, the connection of MO and MI are written so that the right pins can be connected together.
   MI <-> MISO, MO <-> MOSI

2. Read/Write in SPI is performed as a parallel bus control, the serial clock sends the analog signal and the label of
   Master and Slave distinguishes how the write and read are performed between the two parties.
   For something like a display, the serial bus control could help with confirming the signal between two parties.
   For something like an accelerometor, having the check be manual for the engineer should be done as the ADC input happens
   instantaneously with little delay.
